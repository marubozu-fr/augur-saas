Why Augur

Edges you can actually trust

No black boxes, no curve-fit signals. Just transparent conditional statistics with the context you need to size a trade with confidence.

Baseline-relative edges

Every probability is shown next to its unconditional baseline. A 56% hit rate means nothing until you know the base rate — we put both side by side, always.

Sliced every way

Drill into any stat by weekday, gap size, prior-day range, session, or volatility regime. Each slice carries its own sample size so thin buckets never mislead you.

Refreshed daily

Stats recompute automatically after each cash close, rolling in the latest session so your edges reflect this week's market — not a backtest frozen in 2019.

Significance built in

Confidence intervals and minimum-N thresholds keep noise out of the headline number. We grey out edges that aren't statistically meaningful instead of overselling them.
